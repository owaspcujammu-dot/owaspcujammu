Drop team portrait images here, then reference them from lib/site.js, e.g.
photo: "/team/purushottam-k.jpg".

Cards are 3:4 portrait and crop with object-cover, so supply portrait images at
roughly 3:4 - at least 600x800, .jpg or .webp. A square image works but will be
cropped top and bottom, and the face can end up off-centre.
